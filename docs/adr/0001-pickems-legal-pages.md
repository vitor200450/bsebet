# Pickems owns Terms and Privacy; does not inherit bsen.news policies

BSEN Pickems is a separate product under the BSEN fan project umbrella. Legal pages on bsen.news (Wix, editorial, ads) do not cover accounts, predictions, or session data for Pickems. Pickems publishes its own Terms and Privacy in-app, links Supercell's Fan Content Policy in the footer, and acknowledges bsen.news as the parent project without treating the news site's policies as governing this product.
